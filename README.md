When a visit is scheduled, the app learns of it, reminds the patient, and helps the patient choose what to share based on the kind of visit. The app then builds an encrypted link to that package. The link reaches the practice in one of two ways. The app sends it to the practice's EHR through the integration the app already has, or the patient shows a QR code at the desk. Either way, the practice's EHR fetches the package itself.
